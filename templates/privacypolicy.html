Your Privacy Matters

We're committed to protecting your personal information with the highest standards of security and transparency

Last Updated: June 15, 2023

Summary: Mental Health AI Companion collects minimal data necessary to provide our services. We never sell your data. All sensitive information is encrypted. You control what you share.

Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Mental Health AI Companion ("we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, discloses, and safeguards your information when you use our mental health platform and services.

1. Information We Collect

We collect different types of information to provide and improve our services:

Personal Information

  • Account Data: Email address, username, password (hashed)
  • Profile Information: Age, gender (optional), timezone
  • Mood/Journal Entries: Only what you voluntarily provide

Technical Information

  • Device Data: IP address, browser type, operating system
  • Usage Data: Feature usage, session duration
  • Cookies: For functionality and analytics (manage in settings)

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information only for legitimate purposes:

  • Provide and personalize our services
  • Improve mental health recommendations
  • Respond to your requests and questions
  • Analyze usage patterns to enhance features
  • Ensure platform security and prevent abuse
  • Comply with legal obligations

3. Data Sharing & Disclosure

We value your trust and limit data sharing:

  • Never Sold: We don't sell or rent your personal data
  • Service Providers: Only to trusted partners who assist our operations (under strict confidentiality)
  • Legal Requirements: When required by law or to protect rights
  • With Your Consent: Only when you explicitly opt-in

Mental Health Data Protection: Your journal entries and mood data are encrypted end-to-end. Even our team cannot read this sensitive content unless you explicitly share it through our community features.

4. Data Security

We implement robust security measures:

  • End-to-end encryption for sensitive data
  • Regular security audits and penetration testing
  • Two-factor authentication options
  • Employee training on data protection
  • Secure server infrastructure with access controls

5. Your Rights & Choices

You have control over your data:

  • Access: Request a copy of your personal data
  • Correction: Update inaccurate information
  • Deletion: Request deletion of your account and data
  • Export: Download your data in a portable format
  • Opt-Out: From non-essential communications
  • Restrict Processing: Limit how we use your data

6. Children's Privacy

Our services are not designed for children under 16. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 16. If we become aware of such collection, we will take steps to delete the information.

7. International Data Transfers

If we transfer data outside your country, we ensure adequate protection through:

  • Standard contractual clauses
  • Privacy Shield frameworks (where applicable)
  • Other legally-approved mechanisms

8. Policy Changes

We may update this policy periodically. We'll notify you of significant changes through:

  • Email notifications
  • In-app alerts
  • Updated "Last Updated" date
