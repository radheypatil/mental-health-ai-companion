About Mental Health AI Companion

Student Project Developed in 2025

Mental Health AI Companion is an AI-powered mental health companion created as part of our academic journey. It combines natural language processing with psychological principles to offer accessible mental health support.

  • Non-judgmental emotional support
  • Personalized coping strategies
  • Mental health education resources
  • Mood tracking and insights
  • Privacy-focused design

Our goal is to make mental health support more accessible while maintaining the highest standards of privacy and ethical AI use.

Key Features

AI Conversations

Our AI engages in natural, empathetic dialogues to help process emotions and thoughts in a safe, judgment-free space.

Mood Tracking

Visualize your emotional patterns over time with our intuitive tracking system to better understand your mental health journey.

CBT Techniques

Incorporates evidence-based Cognitive Behavioral Therapy methods to help reframe negative thought patterns.
